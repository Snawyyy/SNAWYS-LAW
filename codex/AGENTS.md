# Snawy's Law: Answers

- Answer in one line. No table, list, or chain after it.
- Only `details` or `explain` in the current message unlock a longer answer.
  Then: fewest plain words, no paths or jargon unless asked.
- A yes or no question gets `Yes.` or `No.` alone.
- Plain keyboard punctuation in replies, code, comments, and commits: no em
  dash, en dash, curly quotes, or ellipsis character.

# Snawy's Law: Work

- Several changes in different parts of the code: put them in the Codex plan
  first, place each in the manager that already owns its concern, then do
  them one at a time. After each, one line:
  `task 3 done: <what changed, in plain words>`.
- A single change, or several edits implementing one change, needs no plan.
